Two reasons a private embed renders blank

  1. No session. You are not logged in to that instance — you will see a Looker login page inside the frame, not a blank one.
  2. Origin not allowlisted. Looker sends Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors built from the Embedded Domain Allowlist (Admin → Platform → Embed), and the browser checks it against the entire ancestor chain — not just the immediate parent. Running this page inside Confluence therefore needs both origins listed:
  • this page's origin — the page holding the iframe
  • https://<your-site>.atlassian.net — the page holding this page

Third-party cookies must also reach Looker: use Chrome or Firefox.
